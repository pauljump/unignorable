How it works

🏛
The city's own data
We pull every 311 report — encampment, drug activity, homeless-assist, panhandling — and cluster the thousands of duplicate tickets at one spot into a single Issue with its full history.
🔁
Closed ≠ fixed
We count how often a "resolved" ticket was followed by a new report at the same block. That gap — closed vs. actually fixed — is the story 311 was built to hide.
👁
You make it true
Tap "I see this often" to corroborate. Add a photo. Your verdict — still here, worse, cleaned, gone — overrides the city's "closed." Refreshed from open data daily.
