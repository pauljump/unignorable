How It Works

Four steps to a smarter walk

Enter your addresses, choose what to avoid, and Unignorable builds a walking route that keeps you away from the conditions you care about.

Enter your route

Type or say "Where from?" and "Where to?" — NYC-bounded address search finds your blocks instantly. Tap the GPS button to use your current location.

Choose what to avoid

Toggle avoidance filters for encampments, drug activity, ALPR cameras, broken sidewalks, potholes, illegal dumping, and broken signals. All default off — you add only what matters to you.

Add a stop on the way

Discover live Citi Bike stations near your route with real-time bike and dock counts. Tap any station to shape your walk past it without changing your destination.

Walk with confidence

See your exact route on the map with turn-by-turn directions. One tap opens the route in Apple Maps or Google Maps in walking mode with your stop and shaping points.

Core Features

Everything you can do

A walking-first civic intelligence app that combines avoidance routing, real-time conditions, community verification, and accountability — all on one map.

🛡️

Avoidance Routing

Routes are scored against selected map layers. High-confidence locations become hard exclusions; uncertain ones influence ranking. Walking mode enforces ferry avoidance and rejects routes over 30% time or 35% distance premium.

Share
🧭

Turn-by-Turn Directions

Real-street walking directions with SF Symbols maneuver icons. Small walkway transitions are folded out; real named-street decisions, stairs, ramps, and bridges are highlighted.

Share
📲

Apple & Google Maps Export

One-tap deep links open your generated route in Apple Maps or Google Maps in walking mode — with your intentional stop and up to three shaping waypoints baked in.

Share
📍

Condition Estimates

Every map point shows estimated presence probability, uncertainty range, last agency check, last field observation, and routing impact. Bayesian model with 10-day evidence half-life.

Share
✅

On-the-Ground Verification

When you're physically near an encampment point, report "Still here," "Gone," or "Can't tell." Your proximity is GPS-checked and deduplicated. Two same-day observations become model evidence.

Share
📸

Photo Evidence & Reporting

Snap a photo, pick a status (Still here / Got worse / Cleaned up / Gone), and add a note. Photos are automatically downscaled. A person reviews every submission before it goes public.

Share
🚴

Live Citi Bike Stops

Toggle Citi Bike on the map to see stations near your route with real-time bike and dock counts. Tap any station to add it as an intermediate waypoint — your destination stays the same.

Share
⬆️

Confirm & Upvote

One tap to confirm "I see this." Corroboration counts drive severity ranking and map visual weight. Your confirmation feeds back into the condition model for the whole community.

📋

Route Alternatives

See multiple route options ranked by avoidance effectiveness. Each shows duration, distance, and how many high-confidence locations are crossed. Pick the one that fits.

Map Layers

Seven layers of city intelligence

Each layer is sourced from public data — OpenStreetMap, NYC 311, and live GBFS feeds — refreshed daily and never stale.

License plate cameras (ALPR)
Community-maintained ALPR locations from OpenStreetMap. Shows manufacturer when tagged (e.g. Flock Safety). 45 m routing buffer.
Encampment reports
NYC 311 encampment clusters with probabilistic presence model. High-probability sites with recent corroboration are hard exclusions. 55 m buffer.
Drug activity
Indoor and outdoor drug activity 311 report clusters. Recurring clusters with 5+ reports at a site. 110 m buffer for broad clusters.
Illegal dumping
Repeat illegal dumping sites from 311 data. Shows recurrence pattern and agency response history.
Broken sidewalks
Hazardous sidewalk conditions from 311 reports. Especially relevant for accessibility and walking safety.
Bad roads
Potholes, cave-ins, and street conditions from 311 data. Shows how long the condition has persisted.
Broken lights & signals
Broken street lights and pedestrian signals. Critical for nighttime walking safety.

Privacy & Data

What we don't collect

Unignorable is built on public data and respects your privacy by default.

🔒

No Trip History

The server does not store routes or trip history. Addresses persist only in your browser's local storage.

📍

GPS Optional

Manual A→B entry works without geolocation permission. GPS is only requested for "use current location" and field observations.

📡

Public Sources Only

All map data comes from public sources: OpenStreetMap, NYC 311, and GBFS feeds. No private or proprietary surveillance data.
