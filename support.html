How can we help?

Curbnote is free early access for NYC walks and recurring block conditions.

A route isn't working

Enter both addresses and choose an address suggestion. Try fewer avoidance preferences if no practical route is available. Check your connection and try again. Only NYC walking routes are part of this launch.

The map doesn't match the street

Public reports can be old or approximate. Open the condition and choose a nearby check only when you are physically there. A submitted check waits for review. Conditions never establish a live safety guarantee.

Tell us what happened

Send feedback or request support. Keep your receipt link to see our reply; no account is required. We aim to review launch feedback every weekday, but this is not an emergency service.

For emergencies, use local emergency services. For a city service request, use NYC 311 directly; product feedback does not file a city complaint.
