Description:

Unignorable helps you plan a walk through New York City with more context about the blocks along the way.

Choose a start and destination, select the reported conditions you want to walk around, and compare practical walking routes. Read the evidence behind a mapped condition, see the dates and limitations, and share a block record with a neighbor.

When you're nearby, you can submit a condition check for review. Public reports are approximate and may be out of date; they are not live safety information. A closed city ticket is not proof that a condition has stayed resolved.

Built natively for iPhone with MapKit, native search, directions and sharing. Apple Maps and Google Maps handoff are available, though those apps may recalculate the route. No account is needed for walking or product feedback.

Tell us what worked and what should change. Your feedback goes to a private product inbox, and your receipt lets you see our status and reply.

NYC only. Free early access. Not an emergency service or a guarantee of street conditions.
