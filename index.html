What Unignorable does

Unignorable turns one recurring civic condition into one visible loop: public evidence detects it, a nearby person checks it, the permanent record drives accountable action, and people confirm the outcome. Routes and sharing recruit the next useful check or action; they are not separate products.

The current-condition estimate combines recurrence, evidence age, and public agency observations when available. Its numeric output is an uncalibrated heuristic score, not a measured probability or proof that the condition is there now. A reporting window describes only when historical reports most often arrived. It does not predict when people will be present.

Verify conditions only. Do not photograph or characterize people. Nearby community submissions enter a human review queue and do not automatically change the forecast. Approved checks become calibration evidence for a later, versioned model evaluation. No layer identifies an individual.

Routes, complete public records, static license plate-camera observations, Citi Bike stations, and all source data remain available as supporting tools.

Address searches go to OpenStreetMap Nominatim; routing uses public Valhalla. Your regular-route settings are saved only in this browser. The exact generated route can be downloaded as GPX; Apple and Google may recalculate exported routes.
