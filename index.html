What this map means

Unignorable combines public map observations with recurring NYC 311 reports. It shows license plate cameras, homelessness and encampment reports, drug activity reports, dumping, broken sidewalks, bad roads, and broken lights or signals. “Flock Safety” appears only when the public manufacturer tag says Flock.

Selected locations become street exclusions during driving or walking route generation. Routes update automatically. Walking candidates avoid ferries and are rejected when avoidance would add more than 30% travel time or 35% distance. Public reports can be wrong or stale; no layer identifies an individual or proves a condition is present now.

When 311 resolution text explicitly says NYPD or DHS responded, the popup says so and gives the count. Address searches go to OpenStreetMap Nominatim; routing uses public Valhalla. Your regular-route settings are saved only in this browser.

The exact generated line stays visible here and can be downloaded as GPX. Google and Apple receive an intentional stop plus up to three shaping points, but either app can recalculate.

Browsing the map is free. A one-time $1 pass unlocks routes for 24 hours on this browser; a one-time $25 pass unlocks them for one year.
